Ads (Google AdSense) Settings

Fill these in once your Google AdSense / Ad Manager application is approved. Leave empty and the app runs ad-free with a "Ads not configured yet" placeholder — nothing breaks while you wait for approval.

Google AdSense Publisher ID

Format: ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX — found in AdSense → Account → Settings → Account information.

Interstitial Ad Slot ID

The ad shown right before a book opens. Create this ad unit in AdSense → Ads → By ad unit → Display ads.

Rewarded Ad Unit ID (Google Ad Manager)

Powers "watch ad → earn 1 key". Requires a Google Ad Manager account with a Rewarded ad unit created — separate from basic AdSense.

Note: the secret Server-Side Verification key Google gives you for Rewarded Ads is NOT entered here — it must be stored only in the backend (Cloud Functions config), never in a field visible to the browser, or it could be read by anyone.

AI Mode Settings

Paste the URL of your AI proxy (e.g. your Cloudflare Worker). The real AI provider key stays hidden on the proxy itself — it is never entered here or stored in this app, so it can never be exposed to users.

Past Paper — Exam Years Settings

Set the most recent academic year shown in Past Paper Mode (e.g. 2026 shows "Academic Year 2026/2025"). The platform automatically lists this year and the four before it (5 total).

Manage Subject Materials

Choose a subject, then add documents/books/videos for it. New uploads appear after the last one on the subject's page for real users.
